Welcome to the official blog for **Pretty-console**!

## What is Pretty-console?

The fluent, zero-cost terminal styling toolkit for Rust: spans, lines, panels, gradients, hyperlinks and ANSI-aware text tools.

## What to expect here

This blog is the central hub for everything related to Pretty-console. We will be publishing:

- **Deep dives** into the architecture, design decisions, and inner workings of the project.
- **Tutorials and how-to guides** to help you get up and running quickly.
- **Release notes** and feature announcements as the project evolves.

Whether you are just discovering Pretty-console or have been using it for a while, we are glad you are here. Stay tuned for our first official deep dive!
